# MGX FLAPPY REMASTERED

A modern take on the classic flappy formula, brought to you by MGX INTERACTIVE.
Tap. Fly. Survive. Sounds simple, right? It's not. MGX FLAPPY REMASTERED takes the one-button arcade formula and cranks it up with powers, four completely different maps, and low-gravity space mode. Can you beat your own high score?

## ABOUT THE GAME
MGX FLAPPY REMASTERED is a fast-paced, one-button arcade game. You control a bird, you tap to flap, you dodge pipes. That's the core. But that's just the beginning.
The longer you survive, the more chaos you face. The day turns to night. The whole world changes around you. And when you think you've seen it all, you unlock the lava map and space map - where gravity stops working the way it should.
This is not just a Flappy Bird clone. This is Flappy Bird with attitude.

## FEATURES
4 unique maps to unlock and play:
- **CLASSIC** - the familiar blue sky, green pipes, and classic vibe
- **NEON** - a neon-soaked night world with purple pipes and glowing stars
- **LAVA** - scorching hot world with lava-colored pipes and a burning bird
- **SPACE** - low gravity, asteroid debris, planet with rings, and an astronaut bird

3 power-ups to help you survive:
- **SHIELD (Q)** - 15 seconds of invincibility, 30 second cooldown
- **FREEZE (E)** - freezes the world for 5 seconds, 30 second cooldown
- **DESTROY (F)** - destroys the 3 nearest pipes in front of you, 30 second cooldown

Difficulty selection:
- **EASY** - slow and forgiving
- **NORMAL** - the standard experience
- **HARD** - faster pipes, tighter gaps
- **EXTREME** - for the truly brave

Other features:
- Day and night cycle - the world changes automatically while you play
- Dynamic commentary - the bird talks to you when you fail, when you succeed, and when things get intense
- Particle effects - sparks and explosions
- Retro beep sounds for every action
- Full English and Polish language support
- Animated MGX INTERACTIVE intro
- Custom options menu and clean HUD

## HOW TO PLAY
- **LEFT CLICK** - flap
- **Q** - activate shield
- **E** - activate freeze
- **F** - destroy 3 pipes ahead
- **P or ESC** - pause
- **R** - restart after crashing
- **Arrows / WASD** - move through menus

Your goal: survive. Dodge the pipes. Use your powers wisely. Beat your best score.

## MAPS
**CLASSIC** - Green pipes, blue sky, sunny day. Where every player starts.
**NEON** - Purple pipes, glowing stars, deep night. Unlocked after scoring 5 points.
**LAVA** - Scorching hot world, lava-colored pipes, burning bird. Unlocked after scoring 15 points.
**SPACE** - Low gravity, floating debris, a planet with a ring in the sky. The bird is an astronaut. Unlocked after scoring 30 points.
Each map has its own atmosphere and its own tricks.

## DIFFICULTY
**EASY** - Slower pipes, wider gaps, relaxed pace. Great for learning.
**NORMAL** - The standard MGX FLAPPY experience.
**HARD** - Faster pipes, tighter gaps. Requires focus but fair.
**EXTREME** - Fast pipes, tight gaps. For the truly brave.

## TIPS
- Stay calm. Panic leads to death.
- Save your shield for emergencies.
- Use freeze when pipes are too close together.
- Use destroy when you're about to crash - it removes 3 pipes ahead.
- Practice on CLASSIC before jumping to higher difficulties.
- Score 30 to unlock SPACE - it's worth it.
